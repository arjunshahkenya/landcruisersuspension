How the physics works

The body can rise, fall and pitch. Each axle moves against springs and dampers, and tires can lose contact and land again. Tire radius and the length of the contact patch determine how each tire climbs the bump. Both wheels on an axle follow the same road profile, with the rear axle one wheelbase behind the front.

The stiffness control scales the front and rear effective wheel rates together. Damping and tire response are estimated. The charts measure motion from the loaded resting position.

Speed stays constant throughout each pass. The time dial changes playback only. The calculation stops if a severe impact exhausts the estimated tire, suspension or body clearance, or goes beyond the pitch model. Steering, braking and roll are outside this model.
